Post Studio

Post Studio is a personal desktop tool made by Julian Green. It runs on his own computer and helps him publish his own short videos: it suggests titles, descriptions and hashtags, and uploads and schedules the videos to his own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

It is not a public service. Nobody else's account is used, and there is no website or server behind it: everything happens on the computer it runs on.

What it connects to

  • YouTube (Google): to upload his videos, edit their titles, descriptions, tags and schedule, and read his own channel's view counts and watch time.
  • TikTok: to send his videos to his TikTok drafts.
